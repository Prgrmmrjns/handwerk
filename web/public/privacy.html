Privacy Policy

Handwerk is an educational 3D hand-anatomy and injury visualisation app (web and Android) by Jonas. It has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.

What the app does on your device

  • Loads a local 3D hand model and scene presets stored in the app / website bundle.
  • Keeps UI state (selected preset, playhead, camera) in memory on your device only.
  • Does not require you to sign in.

Optional network request (natural-language parse)

If you type a free-text request (for example “displaced scaphoid with Herbert screw”) and tap Show, Handwerk first runs an on-device offline rule parser. When a network connection is available, the app may also send that request text to POST https://handwerk-ruddy.vercel.app/api/parse so an optional language-model helper can refine the scene. If that call fails or you are offline, the app keeps the offline result.

The request body contains only the text you typed (and no account ID). Server logs on the hosting provider (Vercel) may retain standard HTTP metadata (timestamp, IP address, user agent) as part of normal infrastructure logging. Do not enter names, medical record numbers, or other personal health identifiers into the request field.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or profile.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone.
  • No crash/analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, etc.).
  • No advertising or cross-app tracking.

Children

Handwerk is intended for adults studying anatomy or trauma care. It is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Handwerk is for education only. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not clinical advice. Fracture geometry is illustrative.
